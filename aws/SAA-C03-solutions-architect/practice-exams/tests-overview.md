## Simulation & Readiness Benchmarks

Before scheduling the real 130-minute exam (65 questions, passing score 720/1000):

1. **Practice Scoring**: Consistently score 80%+ on unseen timed 65-question full exams, with no single domain below 75%.
2. **Pacing Target**: Complete first pass of 65 questions in 110 minutes or less (~1.5 minutes/question), reserving 20 minutes for flagged multi-response items.
3. **Multiple-Response Mastery**: Achieve 70%+ accuracy on choose-TWO / choose-THREE items (all-or-nothing scoring).
4. **Error Log Discipline**: For every missed question, log the task statement, why the correct architecture fits every stem constraint, and which keyword or trap caused the wrong choice; re-test missed questions after 3 days.
5. **External Calibration**: Complete the free AWS Official Practice Question Set and timed full-length AWS Official Practice Exam on [AWS Skill Builder](https://explore.skillbuilder.aws/) to calibrate against live exam wording.
